# What we do

We apply microcement — a thin, seamless cementitious finish — directly
over existing surfaces. The result is a continuous, modern look without
demolition.

## Where it works

- **Bathrooms** — walls, floors and shower areas. Waterproof, joint-free.
- **Kitchens** — countertops, backsplashes, floors.
- **Floors** — residential and light commercial. 3 mm thin, no level changes.
- **Walls** — accent walls or full rooms, indoor or covered outdoor.
- **Furniture** — built-in cabinetry, vanities, custom pieces.

## How it works

We work directly with you and your contractor or designer. Typical project
flow: site visit → finish sample → preparation → application (3–5 days) →
final sealing.
